# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, harassment-free community regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, precise, and empathetic. Welcome questions, critique ideas rather than people, disclose conflicts, respect privacy, and accept responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing, or sustained disruption is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these standards, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method published in the repository hosting profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical; the project must publish a dedicated conduct contact before accepting public community contributions.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
